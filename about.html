Software made useful.

Soheili Labs is a software studio in Plano, Texas. We build focused tools that do one job well: AgencyOS for running an agency, RecruitHelper for screening and hiring, Soheili Labs AI for getting a real business website in minutes, and Redirect Rehearsal for checking site migrations. Plain pricing, no per-seat fees, real support.

What we make

Soheili Labs is an independent software studio based in Plano, Texas, building practical tools for small businesses and the teams behind them. Our products include AgencyOS, a single workspace for an agency's clients, projects and approvals; RecruitHelper, which brings candidate screening, video interviews and the hiring pipeline into one place for a flat monthly price; Soheili Labs AI, which writes a complete website from a plain description of your business; Redirect Rehearsal, a client-ready check of every old URL after a website migration; and FaithFinder, a free app for finding a nearby place of worship. Every product is built to be simple to start, honest about what it costs, and backed by a real person at support@soheililabs.com.

Why Soheili Labs exists

Software often becomes harder to use as it grows. Our approach is the opposite: define the job clearly, make the product fast and understandable, and keep unnecessary friction out of the way.
