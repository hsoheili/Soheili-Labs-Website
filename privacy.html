PRIVACY

Privacy should be understandable.

This page states the current privacy posture for the Soheili Labs portfolio site and points users to product-specific policies where appropriate.

SoheiliLabs.com

The portfolio site does not use accounts, forms, cookies, or analytics. It collects no personal information beyond the standard request logs kept by its web host. If that changes, this page will name the provider and the data collected.

ClipKeep

ClipKeep is designed around local clipboard-history storage on the user's Mac. Sensitive-app exclusions and pause controls reduce the chance of capturing sensitive content, but no clipboard manager can guarantee that sensitive content will never be captured.

AgencyOS

AgencyOS is a separate web application with its own privacy policy covering accounts, application data, cookies, hosting, and the services it uses. Read the AgencyOS privacy policy.

RecruitHelper

RecruitHelper is a separate web application with its own privacy policy covering recruiter accounts, candidate responses, cookies, hosting, and the services it uses. Read the RecruitHelper privacy policy.

LinguaFlow and RPG MasterTool

These products are in development and are not yet available to the public. Each will have its privacy details published before it is released.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@soheililabs.com.
